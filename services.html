What we do

From first line
to final handover.

We offer five overlapping services. Most commissions use all of them; some clients arrive for a single one. All are led by a partner and lived with to the end.

01

Full Architectural Design

Concept through completion — brief, planning, technical design, tender and site supervision. The whole building, one team.

  • RIBA Stages 0–7
  • Planning & building regs
  • Contract administration
02

Interior Architecture

Joinery, lighting, material palettes and furniture — the interior considered as architecture, not decoration.

  • Bespoke joinery
  • Lighting design
  • Furniture sourcing
03

Planning & Heritage

Listed buildings, conservation areas and tricky sites. We negotiate with authorities and write the statements that win consent.

  • Heritage statements
  • Planning apps
  • Appeals
04

Feasibility & Strategy

Before you commit — site appraisal, massing options, cost corridors and a clear yes/no on what the land can hold.

  • Site appraisal
  • Massing studies
  • Cost advice
05

Consultation & Crit

For self-builders and other practices — a half-day crit of your drawings, your site, or your stalled project.

  • Design crit
  • Material clinics
  • Self-build support
