Before you write

Quick answers

Do you charge for the first call?

No. The first conversation is always free, whether it leads to a feasibility study or nothing at all.

How soon can you start?

We typically have a 3–4 month lead time. Feasibility studies can often start sooner.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes, happily, before we discuss sensitive sites or commercial briefs.

Do you work internationally?

Selectively. We have current projects in the UK, Ireland and Portugal.
